# Asset credits

## Sound effects synthesised in-repo --- `audio/catch_blue.wav`, `audio/hit_orange.wav`

Synthesised from scratch in Python (stdlib `wave`/`struct`/`math`, raw
sine/sawtooth waveforms with a hand-tuned envelope --- see
`scripts/gen_riff_sfx.py`) --- not sampled or sourced from anywhere, so no
third-party licence applies.
